Privacy policy

Test version, to be reviewed before launch.

Gospel33 provides this order tracking service to its clients. When you sign in with Google and choose to connect Gmail, you grant us read-only access to your Gmail account.

What we access

We search only for order-related emails (such as orders, invoices and receipts). Authorized staff can view those messages to track and support your orders. We never send, delete or change your email.

How we use it

Gmail data is used only to provide order tracking to you. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except as required by law. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Your Google access token is encrypted at rest. We do not store copies of your emails; they are fetched from Google when viewed. All staff access is logged.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time from the home page, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting revokes our access immediately.
